Keep a folder the same on your phone and your computers, over your own Wi-Fi. Nothing goes to the cloud: no account, no server, the files go straight from one device to the other, encrypted.

Tandem shows every change before it makes it, and never overwrites or deletes a file without keeping a copy.

How it works
• Choose a folder on the phone, and its copy on a computer (an empty folder is fine).
• Pair them once: scan the QR code the computer shows.
• Sync: Tandem compares the two copies and lists what it will do: files to send, to receive, deleted on the other side, and files changed on both sides, with three answers each: this device's version, the other's, or both. Text files can be compared side by side first.
• Between syncs, the home screen says what a sync would do ("2 to send · 1 to receive"), or why it can't tell. It never says "in sync" without seeing both sides.
• Every replaced or deleted file is kept for 90 days in the folder's old versions, and can be put back.
• Several folders, several devices: each copy is synced on its own.

Good to know
• The other side is the Tandem desktop app (Linux, Windows, macOS), free at https://sigmasd.github.io/tandem/ Both must be on the same network, with Tandem open, to sync.
• It does not sync in the background: you start each sync and see it. On the phone it works while the app is open.
• To reach your folders, the app asks for "All files access".
• It can't open files in other apps yet.
• Empty folders, links and file permissions are not synced.

Tandem is free software (GPL-3.0-or-later). The Android app runs Tandem's core with Node.js on the phone.
